## 🎓 Official Exam Rules Implemented

### SSC CHSL/CGL:
- Speed: 35 WPM (English), 30 WPM (Hindi)
- Error Formula: Net = Gross - (Errors × 10)
- Backspace: Full
- Highlight: Word + Error

### Railway RRB NTPC:
- Speed: 30 WPM (English), 25 WPM (Hindi)
- Error Formula: Net = Gross - (Errors × 2)
- Backspace: Disabled
- Highlight: Letter by letter

### UPSSSC:
- Speed: 30 WPM (English), 25 WPM (Hindi)
- Error Formula: Net = Gross - Errors
- Backspace: Current + Previous word
- Highlight: Blue on correct

### Allahabad High Court:
- Speed: 35 WPM (English), 30 WPM (Hindi)
- Error Formula: Net = Gross - (Errors × 5)
- Backspace: Current word only
- Highlight: Word + Error
